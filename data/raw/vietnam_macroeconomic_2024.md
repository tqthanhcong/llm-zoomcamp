# Vietnam Macroeconomic Overview 2024

**Document type:** Educational sample based on public macroeconomic releases  
**Primary institution:** General Statistics Office of Vietnam (GSO)  
**Reference:** https://www.gso.gov.vn/en/data-and-statistics/2025/01/socio-economic-situation-in-the-fourth-quarter-and-2024/  
**Coverage:** Calendar year 2024

## Economic growth

Vietnam's gross domestic product increased by 7.09% in 2024 compared with 2023. Growth accelerated during the year, and fourth-quarter GDP rose by 7.55% year on year. Agriculture, forestry and fisheries expanded by 3.27%; industry and construction grew by 8.24%; and services increased by 7.38%. The service sector made the largest contribution to the increase in total value added, while manufacturing remained an important industrial driver.

## Prices and domestic demand

The average consumer price index increased by 3.63% in 2024, remaining below the National Assembly's inflation ceiling. Core inflation averaged 2.71%. Retail sales of consumer goods and services grew by 9.0% in nominal terms. After adjusting for prices, real retail sales growth was lower, showing why analysts should distinguish nominal demand from purchasing-power growth.

## Trade and external balance

Total merchandise trade reached approximately USD 786.3 billion. Exports were about USD 405.5 billion and imports about USD 380.8 billion, producing a merchandise trade surplus of roughly USD 24.8 billion. The foreign-invested sector continued to account for a large share of both exports and imports. The United States was the largest export market, while China was the largest import market.

## Investment and business conditions

Realized foreign direct investment was estimated at USD 25.35 billion, the highest annual disbursement in the available series. Total registered FDI was approximately USD 38.23 billion. Public investment implementation continued to support infrastructure and aggregate demand, although disbursement performance differed across ministries and localities. Newly registered and reactivated enterprises coexisted with a substantial number of firms temporarily suspending operations or leaving the market.

## Labour market and interpretation

The labour force aged 15 and above was about 53.0 million people. Employment was about 51.9 million, and the working-age unemployment rate was close to 2.24%. Labour-market aggregates improved alongside output, but job quality and productivity remained important structural issues. These annual figures describe the aggregate economy and should not be treated as forecasts or as evidence about every province or household.
