# Vietnam Fiscal and Tax Support Measures 2024

**Document type:** Educational policy summary  
**Primary institutions:** Government of Vietnam and Ministry of Finance  
**Reference:** https://www.mof.gov.vn/  
**Coverage:** Selected fiscal and tax measures applying in 2024

## Value-added tax reduction

Vietnam continued the two-percentage-point value-added tax reduction for eligible goods and services normally subject to the 10% rate. For eligible transactions, the applicable rate was 8%. The measure excluded several categories specified by the government, including parts of telecommunications, finance, banking, securities, insurance, real estate, metals, mining products and goods or services subject to special consumption tax. Businesses had to verify eligibility rather than apply the reduced rate universally.

## Tax and land-rent deferrals

The government extended deadlines for selected value-added tax, corporate income tax, personal income tax and land-rent payments for eligible enterprises, organizations, households and individuals. A deferral changes the timing of cash payments but does not normally eliminate the underlying tax liability. Its immediate benefit is liquidity: an eligible business keeps cash longer and can use it for payroll, inventories or working capital before the deferred amount becomes due.

## Environmental protection tax on fuel

Reduced environmental protection tax rates on petrol, oil and lubricants continued during 2024. The measure was intended to moderate fuel costs for households and businesses and thereby reduce cost pressure across transport and production chains. Its fiscal cost must be weighed against the short-run support to consumption, logistics and price stability.

## Public investment and demand support

Accelerating public investment remained a central fiscal priority. Transport and other infrastructure spending can support current demand while expanding productive capacity over time. The economic effect depends on project readiness, procurement, site clearance, implementation quality and the domestic content of spending. Slow disbursement delays the intended stimulus even when a large budget has already been authorized.

## Policy interpretation

Tax reductions, payment deferrals and public investment affect the economy through different channels. A tax-rate cut reduces the amount payable on eligible activity; a deferral mainly shifts payment through time; and public investment creates direct government demand. These measures can complement monetary policy, but poorly targeted relief or low-quality investment can raise fiscal costs without creating durable productivity gains.
